\section{Šta je urađeno}
Svaki MIDI prolazi \textbf{Factory + Gold MAX}:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Velocity} --- samo Factory profil (\texttt{PROFILE\_ONLY}). Gold i neural \emph{ne} smiju dirati velocity.
  \item \textbf{Sve ostalo što evidencija dozvoli} --- Gold: groove, drum/bass, strum, fill sadržaj, solo, Expression CC11, ukrasi.
  \item \textbf{Factory veto} --- Pa800 struktura, Guitar Mode, PowerChord voicing, Brass, Strings/Pad, RX/DNC.
\end{itemize}

Izlaz je podijeljen \textbf{po 100 fajlova}:
\texttt{output/factory\_gold\_batches/batch\_001/} \ldots{}
Uz svaku grupu ide \texttt{batch\_00N.tex} (katalog) i \texttt{batch\_00N.json}.

\section{Jedna rečenica za Drive / katalog}
Kopiraj ovo uz svaki batch zip:

\begin{verbatim}
PA800 Factory+Gold MAX, batch NNN, 100 MIDI.
Velocity = Factory profil (ne Gold, ne AI).
Ritam/gate/groove/strum/fill/solo/CC11 = Gold gdje je evidencija.
RX/DNC i Voice = Factory. Hardware A/B nije rađen.
\end{verbatim}
